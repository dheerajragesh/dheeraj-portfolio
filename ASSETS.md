# Portfolio assets

- `assets/images/profile.jpg` — your main portfolio photo. Recommended: 1200 × 1500 px, JPG or WebP, clear upper-body portrait.
- `assets/certificates/` — place certificate images or PDFs here.
- `assets/resume/` — optional local copy of your résumé. The portfolio currently links to your Google Drive résumé.

## Naming suggestions

- `profile.jpg`
- `bca-certificate.pdf`
- `full-stack-certificate.pdf`

When adding certificates to the portfolio, use the Credentials card in `index.html` and link to files inside `assets/certificates/`.
